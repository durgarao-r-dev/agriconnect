About AgriConnect

A portal built from the field up

AgriConnect was created as a single, no-nonsense place for farmers to check rates, manage their own produce listings, and access basic agri-services — without wading through ads or jargon. Admins keep the data live and accurate; farmers stay in control of what they list.

  • Transparent rates — sourced from verified mandi data, updated by admins.
  • Farmer-first listings — add or remove your produce in seconds.
  • No clutter — no AI gimmicks, just the tools you'll actually use.
